Problem Statement (1/2)

Predicting USD/INR Closing Price for a Quant Strategy

Objective & Importance

Predict the next-day (T+1) USD/INR daily closing price and convert forecasts into reliable long/short signals for a systematic trading strategy.

Even a small directional edge improves PnL after costs in this high-liquidity FX pair. Accurate forecasts are critical for hedging (importers/exporters) and risk budgeting.

Market Context & Dynamics

  • ▪Regime Shifts: Driven by RBI policy interventions, crude oil shocks, and global USD cycles (DXY).
  • ▪Autocorrelation: USD/INR exhibits autocorrelation mostly in returns rather than absolute levels.
  • ▪Anomalies: Significant calendar/holiday effects require robust preprocessing.

Technical Challenges & Constraints

  • ▪Modeling: Non-stationarity, structural breaks, overlapping macro drivers, and noisy signals necessitate strict validation to prevent look-ahead bias.
  • ▪Execution: Transaction costs, slippage, and position limits must be modeled.
  • ▪Market Structure: Discrepancy between Indian exchange-trading hours and global FX 24x5 markets creates gap risks.

Success Criteria

Directional Accuracy

RMSE / MAE / MAPE

AUC on Up/Down signals

Net PnL & CAGR

Maximum Drawdown
